# Pop Squadron — Campaign Sectors and Boss Concepts v1.1

**Status:** Approved campaign design decisions
**Originally approved:** October 19, 2026  
**Revised:** October 22, 2026
**Scope:** Campaign 1 — The Balloon Invasion

## Story premise

Balloons lost by children at parks, zoos, beaches, playgrounds, and other places around the world have mysteriously mutated in the wild. They have developed intelligence, unusual abilities, and a plan to take over the world. Pop Squadron pilots must fight the expanding balloon invasion.

This is the approved *story premise*, not a finalized two-paragraph opening script. Narrative copy and mission briefings may be developed separately.

## Approved campaign structure

- **Campaign 1:** Ten Earth-based sectors, with **ten missions per sector** (100 missions total).
- **Boss placement:** The tenth mission of each sector is its boss encounter.
- **Hangar navigation direction:** Select a sector, then display only that sector's ten missions rather than one list of all 100.
- **Progression:** Preserve the existing mission progress, equipment, wallet, and offline functionality when sector navigation is implemented.
- **Future growth:** The design should permit more sectors later without requiring a redesign of navigation or mission data.

### Approved October 22 revisions

- **Sector 4:** Rename Beach to **Beach Boardwalk** to emphasize a believable origin for lost children's balloons: oceanfront boardwalk, pier, amusement attractions and seaside vendors. Retain the **Inflatable Sea Monster** boss concept.
- **Sector 5:** Replace Desert with **Carnival**. A carnival/midway is another natural source of lost balloons and offers distinct tents, rides and game-booth scenery. **Retire the Heat-Resistant Balloon boss concept for this sector**; no replacement boss is approved yet.
- **Narrative progression:** Early sectors feature familiar places where balloons are lost and mutate; later sectors show their spread into remote regions, infrastructure and ultimately an organized invasion. Distinguish Beach Boardwalk's coastal setting from Carnival's midway atmosphere.
- All other sectors, mission ranges and existing boss concepts remain unchanged.

## Design implications

- Each sector can establish its own environment, balloon mutations, and visual theme.
- Boss encounters should be mechanically distinctive, rather than simply larger balloons with more health. Specific mechanics require later design approval.
- Sector navigation should remain manageable on an iPhone and support at least ten sectors.
- Build and verify navigation as a small increment before expanding the first sector's mission roster.
- Existing Missions 1–30 and their implemented bosses are to be preserved; design and implement later sectors in approved, testable increments.

## Possible future expansion — not part of Campaign 1

Surviving or multiplying balloons might travel into space, allowing later campaigns in orbit, on the Moon, or elsewhere in the solar system. This is an **expansion idea**, not an approved list of sectors or implementation scope.

## Change control

Do not rename approved sectors or replace approved boss concepts without a new explicit design decision. The October 22, 2026 changes to Sectors 4 and 5 are explicitly approved and recorded above. This document records campaign design; it does **not** authorize gameplay code changes, artwork generation, or mission implementation.
