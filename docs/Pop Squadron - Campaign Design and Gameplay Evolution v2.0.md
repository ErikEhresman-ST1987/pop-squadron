# Pop Squadron — Campaign Design and Gameplay Evolution v2.0

**Status:** Consolidated current design reference · October 22, 2026. Supersedes the earlier campaign, variety and power-up documents as the active reference. Earlier files remain available for history.

## Story and campaign
Balloons lost by children in familiar locations drift into the wild, mutate, gain intelligence and organize an invasion. Ten Earth-based sectors have ten missions each, with a boss every tenth mission.

October 22 approved revision: Beach became Beach Boardwalk, with oceanfront attractions and pier scenery; Desert became Carnival, with midway attractions. The Desert boss concept was retired. Beach Boardwalk retains the Inflatable Sea Monster concept. Later sectors show the invasion spreading into remote places, infrastructure and major cities. Individual future boss mechanics require approval.

## Difficulty and variety
**Create 100 missions worth playing, not 100 increasingly punishing missions.** Build and playtest one sector at a time; rebalance the full campaign after all ten sectors. Difficulty may rise and fall, and a memorable boss need not be harder than the preceding mission. Evaluate **challenge, variety and enjoyment** separately.

Four complementary systems:
1. **Enemy evolution:** different movement patterns, weaving, formations, durability and tactics, rather than only more enemies or speed.
2. **Player progression:** improved darts, specialized equipment and meaningful hangar choices. Consult the existing weapon registry before proposing a new weapon; several advanced systems already exist. Do not require ownership of all gear to complete a mission.
3. **Helium-cloud advantages:** occasional optional bonuses in selected missions.
4. **Environmental obstacles:** sparse scenery intrusions adding navigation choices and fair collision risks.

## Helium clouds — agreed direction, mechanics pending
- On selected missions, a small percentage of eligible popped balloons may release a collectible helium cloud.
- Fly through the cloud before it dissipates to collect a positive bonus.
- The bonus must be identifiable before pickup with a distinct readable symbol; color alone is insufficient.
- The intended system offers **three possible bonus types**, but their identities are **not approved**.
- These are not cash rewards, and must not replace pop-credit earnings. Bonuses should never be required to finish a mission.
- **Unapproved candidate effects:** one-hit shield, temporarily faster firing, temporary movement boost, partial hull repair. Four candidates are listed for consideration; this does not approve four bonuses or choose the three.
- **Open decisions:** three exact types, effects, duration, spawn odds, eligible balloon types, eligible missions, cloud lifetime, stacking, visuals and cleanup.
- **Potential implementation, not authorization:** explicit mission flags, eligible pop event, chance roll, pickup lifespan and overlap, temporary effect state and HUD feedback.

## Obstacles — agreed direction, mechanics pending
- Use only on selected missions; keep obstacles sparse and most of the playfield open.
- Possible examples: trees or branches, pier structures, a crane derrick/boom extending into the route, and scenery appropriate to later sectors.
- Collision may create aircraft damage risk, but exact collision response and damage are **not yet approved**.
- Preserve clear safe routes, readable art, fair warning, and usable iPhone touch controls; avoid unavoidable hits.
- **Open decisions:** first mission, art, sizes/hitboxes, placement, movement, collision damage, interaction with balloons and player darts, and testing.
- **Potential implementation, not authorization:** mission-specific obstacle configuration, controlled appearance/removal and route-safety tests.

The earlier illustrative mission bands for cloud and obstacle placement are **not** a fixed schedule.

## Playtest baseline
- **Mission 29:** two consecutive victories; latest with three escaped balloons and one hull hit. Weaving is difficult but beatable. Retain balance.
- **Mission 30 Thornback:** victory with a couple of hull hits; easier than 29, but enjoyable. Artwork, movement and thorn attacks well received. Retain encounter for now.
- Sector 3 is provisionally accepted, pending later campaign-wide balancing.
